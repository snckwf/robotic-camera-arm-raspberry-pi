Remote controller for the camera arm

A bench console with two joysticks and four buttons to move the arm without touching the computer. An Arduino Nano reads the commands and passes them to the Raspberry over the USB cable.

Printed parts

Items 14 and 15 of the parts list: console shell (printed upside down, button face on the bed), bottom with the Nano cradle and button plate. PETG.

Hardware and components

Controller

  • Arduino Nano + USB cable to the Pi — 1
  • KY-023 joystick — 2
  • 12×12×7.3 button + A14 10×10 square cap — 4

Screws and other

  • M3×6 — 10 (joysticks 8, plate 2)
  • M3×8 — 4 (bottom)
  • Cable ties — 2
  • Adhesive feet — 4

Wiring

  • Left joystick → VRx/VRy/SW → A0/A1/D2, +5V/GND → Nano 5V/GND
  • Right joystick → VRx/VRy/SW → A2/A3/D3, +5V/GND → Nano 5V/GND
  • Buttons → D4 tilt up, D5 tilt down, D6 rotate left, D7 rotate right; the other pin to GND
  • Nano → Raspberry Pi → USB cable into a Pi USB port (data and power)

  1. Turn the shell upside down on the table, top face down.
  2. Put the caps on the buttons and insert the 4 buttons into the square seats, cap in the hole.
  3. Lay the plate on the buttons, passing the pins through the slots, and screw it down with 2 M3×6 screws.
  4. Screw the 2 joysticks to the posts with 4 M3×6 screws each, 5-pin connector towards the back (USB window side).
  5. Upload the arm_console.ino sketch to the Nano, then solder the wires (step 2) and put it in the cradle of the bottom, USB socket towards the window; secure it with 2 cable ties through the slots.
  6. Close with the bottom and 4 M3×8 screws, add the 4 adhesive feet and connect the USB cable to a Raspberry port.
Before printing, measure with a caliper the hole spacing of the KY-023 (joy_fori, 20.5 × 26.5) and the height of the metal bracket above the board (joy_alt, 11.5).
STEP 02 / 2

Nano wiring

flexible wiressolderheat-shrink
  1. Left joystick: VRx → A0, VRy → A1, SW → D2, +5V → 5V, GND → GND.
  2. Right joystick: VRx → A2, VRy → A3, SW → D3, +5V → 5V, GND → GND.
  3. Buttons: tilt up → D4, tilt down → D5, rotate left → D6, rotate right → D7. The other pin of each button goes to GND (pins on opposite sides of the button).
  4. No resistors: the inputs use the Nano internal pull-up. No wires to the arm box: the USB cable is enough.
Upload the sketch before closing: the Nano LED blinks slowly when it works and stays on while you press a button. The diagram is above, under «Wiring».

Software

  • Upload arm_console.ino to the Nano with the Arduino IDE (Arduino Nano board, 115200 baud). From the Pi you can also do it with arduino-cli.
  • On the Raspberry install python3-serial, copy controller_braccio.py to /opt/braccio/ and controller-braccio.service to /etc/systemd/system/, then sudo systemctl enable --now controller-braccio.
  • Left joystick: X base, Y shoulder; right: Y elbow. Left joystick button: short press STOP, held 2 s HOME base. Right button: light.
  • If a joint moves the wrong way, change its sign in SEGNO at the top of controller_braccio.py.
